Renew Radar

Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026. Published by JDM Digital Technology Corp.

Summary

Renew Radar keeps your subscription list on your own iPhone or iPad. We do not run servers for the app, we do not have accounts, and we do not collect, sell or share your personal information. This policy explains what that means in detail.

Who we are

Renew Radar is published by JDM Digital Technology Corp, an Ontario, Canada company ("we", "us"). Contact us at support@jdmdigitaltechnologies.com.

What stays on your device

The subscriptions you enter (names, costs, billing cycles, renewal dates, cancel links and reminder settings) are stored only on your device, using Apple's on-device storage. We cannot see them.

Your currency and reminder-time settings are also stored on your device.

Alerts

Renewal alerts are local notifications created and delivered by your device. No push server is involved and no information is sent to us to make them work. You can turn notifications off at any time in iOS Settings.

Screenshots

If you use "Add from screenshot", you choose the pictures. The text in them is read on your device using Apple's Vision framework. The pictures and the text are not uploaded, stored by us or shared with anyone. The app keeps only the subscription details you confirm on the review screen.

Exports

When you export a CSV or PDF, the file is created on your device and handed to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes after that (Files, Mail, AirDrop, another app) is your choice and is governed by that destination's own policies.

Links to other sites

Cancel links and the "Open my Apple subscriptions" shortcut open web pages run by other companies, including Apple. Those sites have their own privacy practices, which we do not control.

Analytics, ads and tracking

The app contains no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking tools.

Purchase

The app is a one-time purchase through the Apple App Store. Apple handles your payment and any purchase records under its own privacy policy. We receive only the summary sales and tax reports that Apple provides to developers, which do not identify you.

Children

Renew Radar is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we do not hold your personal information, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. You control your data on your device. Deleting the app deletes its data. If you contact us, we will use your message only to reply to you.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this policy and the effective date below before the change takes effect in a new version of the app.

Contact

Questions about this policy: support@jdmdigitaltechnologies.com.

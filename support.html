Support

Contact support

Email support@jdmdigitaltechnologies.com. Please say which iPhone or iPad you use, your iOS version (Settings > General > About) and what happened. We aim to reply within two business days.

My alerts did not arrive

Check that notifications are allowed: iOS Settings > Notifications > Renew Radar, then turn on Allow Notifications.

Check Focus and Do Not Disturb. They can silence alerts.

In Renew Radar, open Settings and check the "Send alerts at" time. Alerts are delivered at that time of day.

Open the app now and then. It keeps the next 64 alerts scheduled and tops them up each time you open it.

How do I add subscriptions from my Apple subscriptions list?

Apps cannot read that list directly. Use the shortcut in the + menu to open your Apple subscriptions page, take a screenshot, then choose + > Add from screenshot. The text is read on your device, and you check it before saving. If something is misread, correct it on the review screen or add the subscription by hand.

The screenshot import found nothing

Use a clear, full screenshot of the Subscriptions list that shows lines such as "Renews 14 Nov 2026". Screenshot reading currently works for English. You can always add a subscription by hand with + > Add by hand.

Where is my data? Can I back it up?

Your subscriptions are stored only on your device, so we cannot see or recover them. They are included in your normal iPhone or iPad backup. You can also export a CSV or PDF from the menu at the top left of the list.

Can Renew Radar cancel a subscription for me?

No. It reminds you and opens the page where you cancel. Cancelling is always done with the company that bills you.

Refunds and purchases

The app is a one-time purchase handled by Apple. For a refund, go to reportaproblem.apple.com and choose the purchase. We cannot issue refunds because Apple processes the payment.

Privacy and terms

Your subscriptions stay on your device. Read the full Privacy Policy and Terms of Use in the app under Settings > About, or on our website.
